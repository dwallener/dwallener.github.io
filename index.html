01 / On the bench

A fly compass.
Built out of circuits.

A fruit fly keeps track of which way it faces—even after the lights go out. A bump of neural activity moves around a ring as it turns. We’re building a transistor circuit that does the same basic job, from simulation through custom 3D placement & routing.

323 devices. Eight repeatable Sector blocks. Two full-system layout attempts—including a 201-layer tower.

Simulation and layout work so far. Not fabricated yet. Both full-system routes still have open nets.

02 / Where this is headed

Sentinal Shield. Contract won.

We won the contract for the “upside-down radar.” This research is intended to form part of its cognitive architecture. The compass is our current demonstrator; it isn't a completed radar cognition system.
